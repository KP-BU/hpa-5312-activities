Contemporary Issue Framework Planner

Plan your evidence-based framework before you build your presentation. Complete each section, then generate a summary you can copy or download for your slides.

1. The issue

Name the issue in a few words (for example, rural hospital closures or prescription drug prices).

What is happening, who is affected, and why it matters now. Use at least one data point.

2. Analysis

Link the issue to course concepts: financing and payment, determinants of health, technology, policy history, or system structure.

Who gains, who loses, and who decides? Consider patients, providers, payers, employers, and government.

3. Your framework

Describe the core components of your approach.

List at least two sources (peer-reviewed research, government data, or policy analyses) and what each shows.

4. Trade-offs and implementation

Use the health policy trilemma: what improves, and what might get worse?

Political, financial, legal, or operational obstacles.

5. Measuring success

Which two or three measures would show your framework is working, and over what time frame?

What question will you ask classmates to evaluate when they review your presentation?
